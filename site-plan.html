1. Site Name

DRC Energy & Materials Research Hub

Why this name? Energy and Materials Science in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. It positions the site as a central hub for researchers, students, and professionals interested in these fields. The name is professional, descriptive, and clearly communicates the site's purpose.

Domain Availability (optional):drc-energy-materials-hub.org

2. Site Purpose

The DRC Energy & Materials Research Hub serves as an informational platform dedicated to advancing research and innovation in energy and materials science in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The site provides:

  • Research Showcase: Current research projects in materials science, energy, and petrochemistry conducted at the University of Kinshasa and partner institutions.
  • Publication Archive: A collection of scientific publications, articles, and reports related to energy and materials research in the DRC.
  • Collaboration Hub: Information about research partners, laboratories, and opportunities for collaboration.
  • Researcher Profile: A biography and contact information for Jonathan KOLA MONGENGO, the site creator and a petrochemical engineer and researcher.

The site aims to bridge the gap between the chemical and computer sciences by showcasing how digital solutions can address challenges in the petroleum, energy, and materials industries.

3. Scenarios

Scenario 1

Visitor: A researcher from a European university is looking for partners in Africa a joint research project on sustainable energy materials.

Question:"Which research projects are currently being conducted at the University of Kinshasa in the field of energy materials?"

Answer Found:The Researchpage lists current projects, including "Development of Local Materials for Energy Storage" and "Evaluation of Energy Resources in the DRC."

Scenario 2

Visitor: A Congolese student interested in pursuing a career in petrochemical engineering is looking for information about academic and research opportunities.

Question: "Who is the researcher behind this site, and how can I contact them for collaboration or mentorship?"

Answer Found: The Contact / About page provides a biography of Jonathan KOLA MONGENGO, his academic background, and contact information including email, LinkedIn, and GitHub links.

Scenario 3

Visitor: An industry professional from a mining company is interested in the latest research on critical minerals and materials extraction in the DRC.

Question: "What publications and research findings are available on materials science in the DRC?"

Answer Found: The Research page features a list of publications, including "Innovative Materials for Energy Storage" and "Petrochemical Industry in the DRC."

4. Color Scheme

The following color have been selected to created a professional, trustworthy, and visually cohesive identify for the DRC Energy & Materials Research Hub.

Primary Blue #1a3a5c Heading, buttons, footer, navigation
Accent Gold #c8963e Highlight, accents, icons, emphasis
White #ffffff Background, text on dark backgrounds
Dark Text #333333 Body text, paragraph content

Why these colors?

  • Primary Blue (#1a3a5c):Represent trust, professionalism, and stability ideal for a research-oriented site.
  • Accent Gold (#c8963e): Symbolizes excellence, value, and energy — a nod to the petroleum and energy industries.
  • Accent Green (#2a7f6e): Represents sustainability, growth, and environmental consciousness — relevant for renewable energy and materials research.

5. Typography

The site uses the Roboto font family from Google Fonts for its clean, modern, and highly readable design.

Headings (H1, H2, H3)
Roboto Bold
Used for: Page titles, section headings, navigation
Body Text
Roboto Regular
Used for: Paragraphs, lists, descriptions, form labels
Accent / Special
Roboto Light Italic
Used for: Subtitles, quotes, highlighted text, badges

Why Roboto? Roboto is a highly versatile, modern sans-serif font that offers excellent readability on both desktop and mobile devices. Its multiple weights allow for clear visual hierarchy and a professional, clean aesthetic.
